About

I'm Frank (Qingtao) Liu, an engineering student at the University of Toronto (B.A.Sc. with PEY Co-op, Class of 2027). I recently completed a 12-month PEY Co-op term as a Software Engineer at RBC Capital Markets, where I worked on the migration of a fixed-income risk engine to a Linux and Kubernetes based platform.

My interests sit at the intersection of machine learning and production systems: NLP and large-model engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and quantitative finance infrastructure. I have designed RAG architectures, integrated LLMs such as Qwen and DeepSeek, and built financial risk computation pipelines that bridge C++ pricing libraries with Python through SWIG, all deployed with Docker and Kubernetes.

  • Databases & Frameworks: MySQL, PostgreSQL, Milvus, Elasticsearch, Flask, FastAPI

I care about bridging AI theory with real applications: model optimization, CI/CD automation, and systems that are scalable, reliable, and genuinely useful to the people who depend on them.
